# **Volume of a Cuboid (8 kyu)**

### **Description:**
Bob needs a fast way to calculate the volume of a [<u>rectangular cuboid</u>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectangular_cuboid) with three values: the `length`, `width` and `height` of the cuboid.

Write a function to help Bob with this calculation.
